Cartographer's Guide

The world begins as mist. Regents claim hexes to call land into being — each claim is stamped with the current season and year, and the Chronicle remembers.

Claim 🕯 — pick a province (or make one), then click or drag in the fog. After the first hex, new land must touch existing land: the frontier only moves outward. Release 🌫 returns land to the mist.

Terrain ▲ paints claimed land. Roads 〓 and rivers ≋ follow hex boundaries; roads connect hex centers. Sites ⌂ place cities, castles, standing stones and the like — the site appears at once and a small naming card opens beside it.

Inspect ☉ — click any claimed hex to open its province dossier. Drag to pan (any tool: right-drag or hold Space), scroll to zoom.

Advance Season moves the calendar Spring→Summer→Autumn→Winter and rolls the year after Winter. New claims stamp with the new date. The Chronicle tab lists every season's conquests and can replay the whole reclamation from the first candle.

Hex dossier — inspect a hex to edit its local name, terrain, settlement, resources, conditions, and notes. In live sessions these notes are shared. Rivers now follow hex edges: click a boundary and drag to extend a connected waterway. Existing center-running rivers remain intact. Crossings (B) adds bridges or fords to edge rivers. Erase road clears roads in a hex; Erase river removes individual river edges, or an older river when you click inside its hex.

Province names stay off the terrain by default. Hover over a hex to read its province in the bottom bar. Turn on Show province names on the map in the Province tab when you want labels, including in PNG exports.

Undo / Redo restores an entire brush stroke in solo play. Use Ctrl / ⌘ Z to undo and Ctrl / ⌘ Shift Z to redo. F fits all claimed land. Select a province and choose Locate selected province to find it.

Save file writes the world to a JSON file; Load restores it. The map also autosaves in this browser. Export PNG renders a shareable image.
